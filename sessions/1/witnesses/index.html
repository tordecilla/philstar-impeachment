Cylene May L. Del Campo

Commission on Audit state auditor; prosecution witness

Del Campo described post-audit work by the Commission on Audit and its Intelligence and Confidential Funds Audit Office, including audit observation memoranda, notices of suspension or disallowance, acknowledgement receipts, responses, and supporting records.

00:37:19 · S0010400:39:37 · S00110

She described the stated bases for the later notice as audit matters, not findings adopted by the court.
