Atty. Melvin Matibag

Director of the National Bureau of Investigation; prosecution witness

Matibag testified that he issued Administrative Order No. 11 on April 16 to create an office task force to continue and monitor the investigation.

00:34:07 · S0011100:35:16 · S00119

He described the task force's mandate as monitoring presidential security and seeking to identify the person whom he said the Vice President had contacted; he explained that persons of interest were not necessarily the person contacted.

02:03:14 · S0067702:04:44 · S00682

On the Lascañas document, the chair allowed Matibag to testify only that it was used in the NBI investigation, not to establish the truth of its allegations; the chair also noted that the identified document was unsigned, unnotarized, and not under oath.

01:32:42 · S0053301:38:13 · S00565

The defense recorded that Matibag had no personal knowledge of the first investigation and that his testimony about it was based on NBI records. The chair later granted a defense continuance, leaving cross-examination unfinished.
