02

The big picture

The court heard the direct examination and part of the cross-examination of NBI Director Melvin Matibag on Article IV. Matibag described an ongoing NBI task-force inquiry into alleged threats to the President; the defense challenged relevance, personal knowledge, the treatment of videos and documents, and the reliability of an Arturo Lascañas statement. The court repeatedly limited what the testimony and documents could establish, then continued Matibag's cross-examination to the next sitting.
