Jeremy C. Lotoc

National Bureau of Investigation regional director for BARMM and former Cybercrime Division chief; prosecution witness

Lotoc testified that he could confirm Vice President Duterte uttered the statements shown in the November 23 recording, but he did not personally know the factual circumstances described in them.
